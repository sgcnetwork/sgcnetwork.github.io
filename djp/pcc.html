Your PCC,
step by step.

A South African Police Clearance Certificate is issued by SAPS and may be requested for employment, immigration or study abroad.

01

Confirm the checklist

Common items include an application form, original fingerprints and a copy of your ID or passport. Confirm the current official requirements and how fingerprints must be taken for your location.

02

Prepare your application

Tell us whether you are applying from South Africa or overseas, whether an application is already in progress and when the receiving organisation needs it.

03

Plan international use

Ask the receiving organisation how recent the certificate must be and whether an apostille, attestation or translation is needed. Acceptance requirements can differ.

Timing & delivery

Issuing times depend on SAPS processing and any additional checks. Confirm any expedited option, document handling and return-delivery arrangements before proceeding.
