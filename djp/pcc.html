Police clearance,
explained clearly.

What is a Police Clearance Certificate?

A South African Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) is an official SAPS document reporting the outcome of a criminal-record check. It may be requested when moving overseas or taking up work in another country. Check with the organisation requesting it that you are obtaining the correct document.

How DJP can assist

Start with a review of your requirements. DJP can discuss application preparation, the paperwork you need and the next administrative steps. Before proceeding, agree the precise service, quote, submission arrangements and how you will receive updates. Tell us if you already have an application in progress.

Preparing your paperwork

Expect to arrange fingerprints and identification documents. SAPS provides guidance for applicants in South Africa and abroad, including acceptable fingerprint forms. Use the official checklist for your circumstances and confirm any additional requirements before sending originals. An online enquiry starts the conversation; it does not replace the fingerprint or official application process.

Applying from outside South Africa

If you live abroad, tell DJP your current country and where the certificate will be used. This helps us discuss a practical document route and any collection or delivery arrangements that may be available. Confirm handling instructions before posting your documents.

Urgent requests, costs and delivery

Include your deadline when enquiring. Ask for the available service options and a written breakdown of assistance fees, official charges and delivery costs. Processing depends on the issuing authority and the checks needed for the application. Collection and courier arrangements must be agreed for your location.

Using your certificate overseas

Ask the receiving organisation how recent the certificate must be and whether it needs an apostille, authentication or translation. DJP can discuss these related services as part of your enquiry. Confirm the full document journey before booking travel or committing to a submission date.

FBI USA and Zimbabwe clearance

DJP also accepts enquiries for FBI USA clearance and Zimbabwe clearance. Select the relevant service in the enquiry form so your request is assessed separately from a South African PCC. Describe the document requested and where it will be used.

Start your enquiry

Provide your name, contact details, service, destination and preferred deadline. Keep passport numbers, identity documents, fingerprints and criminal-history information out of the initial message. DJP will discuss the appropriate next steps with you.
