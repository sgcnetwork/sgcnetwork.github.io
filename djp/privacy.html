YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy,
with clarity.

This notice explains how this website handles initial enquiries.

Information you provide

The enquiry form collects your name, email, optional phone number, selected service, destination, preferred deadline and message. Do not submit identity documents, passport numbers, fingerprints or criminal history.

How it is used

Your information is stored in DJP’s enquiry system on SGC Network’s infrastructure, using Supabase, and is available only to the authorised site owner. A notification containing your enquiry is also emailed to DJP’s owner through our transactional email provider, Resend. Replies may be handled through Gmail. Your details are used to review and respond to your request and are not displayed publicly.

Your choices

To request access, correction or deletion of an enquiry, submit a new enquiry identifying your earlier reference and the action requested. Only provide enough information to locate it. Further verification may be needed before a request can be completed.

Browser storage

The public enquiry form does not store your completed form locally. Staff access uses a session token held in browser memory while signed in. This website does not include advertising trackers.

Service boundaries

An enquiry is not a visa application or an agreement to purchase a service. Any further document collection, fees and service terms must be agreed separately.
