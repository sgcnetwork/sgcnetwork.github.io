Visa appeal enquiry support

If you received an unfavourable visa outcome, start by identifying the decision and its reasons. An appeal enquiry should focus on the issues raised, the original application and the documents that may address them. DJP can discuss administrative support and document organisation.

What to prepare

  • Date you received the decision
  • Visa category and application reference
  • Reasons stated in the decision
  • Whether an appeal or another application has already been started

Share a brief outline in the initial enquiry. DJP will discuss the appropriate way to provide supporting paperwork after the request has been reviewed.

The process

Check the applicable deadline promptly with the relevant authority or a qualified immigration professional. Confirm the permitted review route before preparing or lodging paperwork; different decisions can require different responses.

How DJP can assist

Discuss the administrative support you need: organising the request, reviewing the document list, identifying missing information and planning agreed submission, follow-up or delivery arrangements. The available service and any specialist involvement are confirmed for your individual request.

Fees and turnaround

Ask for a written quote setting out DJP assistance fees and any official, third-party or courier charges. Explain your deadline at the start so timing can be considered. Availability, required checks and authority processing affect the overall schedule; your enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Will an appeal change the original decision?

An appeal is assessed on its own merits. Neither lodging an appeal nor using an assistance service guarantees a different outcome.
