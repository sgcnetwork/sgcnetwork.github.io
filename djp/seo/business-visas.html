Business travel visa support

Business travel can involve meetings, conferences or other planned commercial activities. The precise activities matter when reviewing the correct visa route. DJP can discuss organising invitations, trip details and the supporting administrative information.

What to prepare

  • Destination and intended activities
  • Inviting company or event
  • Who is paying for the trip
  • Dates and any existing booking or application

Share a brief outline in the initial enquiry. DJP will discuss the appropriate way to provide supporting paperwork after the request has been reviewed.

The process

Explain what you will actually do during the visit. A business visit and taking up employment are different situations and may require different authorisation.

How DJP can assist

Discuss the administrative support you need: organising the request, reviewing the document list, identifying missing information and planning agreed submission, follow-up or delivery arrangements. The available service and any specialist involvement are confirmed for your individual request.

Fees and turnaround

Ask for a written quote setting out DJP assistance fees and any official, third-party or courier charges. Explain your deadline at the start so timing can be considered. Availability, required checks and authority processing affect the overall schedule; your enquiry does not confirm a booking.

What should my invitation explain?

Ask the relevant authority for its checklist. Your host may need to describe the visit purpose, dates, relationship and funding arrangements.
