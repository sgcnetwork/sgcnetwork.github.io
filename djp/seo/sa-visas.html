South African visa assistance

Plan your South African visa enquiry around the reason for your stay. Tourism, employment, study and joining family can involve different applications, supporting evidence and attendance arrangements. DJP can help organise your enquiry and discuss document preparation for the route you intend to use.

What to prepare

  • Your nationality and country of residence
  • Purpose and expected length of stay
  • Whether this is a first application, renewal or change
  • Any existing application reference or upcoming expiry

Share a brief outline in the initial enquiry. DJP will discuss the appropriate way to provide supporting paperwork after the request has been reviewed.

The process

Explain your circumstances before choosing a category. Ask DJP to confirm the current checklist and the assistance included in your quote. Your initial enquiry is not a submitted DHA application.

How DJP can assist

Discuss the administrative support you need: organising the request, reviewing the document list, identifying missing information and planning agreed submission, follow-up or delivery arrangements. The available service and any specialist involvement are confirmed for your individual request.

Fees and turnaround

Ask for a written quote setting out DJP assistance fees and any official, third-party or courier charges. Explain your deadline at the start so timing can be considered. Availability, required checks and authority processing affect the overall schedule; your enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Can DJP choose my visa category?

Describe your plans so the correct requirements can be reviewed. A complex immigration matter may need a qualified immigration professional.
