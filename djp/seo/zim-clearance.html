Zimbabwe police clearance assistance

Zimbabwe clearance enquiries concern a police certificate issued through the Zimbabwean process. Applicants may need it for a job, relocation or another official request. DJP can review the administrative help you need and discuss the paperwork and handling arrangements for your circumstances.

What to prepare

  • Your current country of residence
  • Purpose and destination of the clearance
  • Whether you already have fingerprints or a completed form
  • Deadline and preferred delivery location

Share a brief outline in the initial enquiry. DJP will discuss the appropriate way to provide supporting paperwork after the request has been reviewed.

The process

Confirm the relevant Zimbabwean checklist and fingerprint form before sending documents. The Zimbabwe Consulate in Johannesburg publishes a police-certificate form. The route and handling arrangements should be agreed for your location.

How DJP can assist

Discuss the administrative support you need: organising the request, reviewing the document list, identifying missing information and planning agreed submission, follow-up or delivery arrangements. The available service and any specialist involvement are confirmed for your individual request.

Fees and turnaround

Ask for a written quote setting out DJP assistance fees and any official, third-party or courier charges. Explain your deadline at the start so timing can be considered. Availability, required checks and authority processing affect the overall schedule; your enquiry does not confirm a booking.

Can I enquire while outside Zimbabwe?

Yes. State where you live so the available application and fingerprint arrangements can be checked for your circumstances.
