Before you apply

Depending on your case, an authority may request a passport, photographs, application forms, travel plans, accommodation details, financial evidence or an invitation. Obtain the current checklist for your exact visa category before submitting documents.

Some applicants must attend an appointment for biometrics or an interview. Appointment availability, entry permission and decisions remain with the relevant authorities. An enquiry on this site is not an official visa application.

03 / PLAN WITH CLARITY

Know what
comes next.

How do I get a quote?

Select a service and tell us your destination, current location and deadline. DJP reviews the request before confirming service availability, scope and a quote. Ask for service, official and delivery charges to be identified before agreeing.

Are urgent requests possible?

Mention your deadline in the enquiry. Available priority options must be confirmed for the specific authority and service. No completion date or visa outcome is guaranteed.

Do I need original documents?

Some processes require originals or specified certified copies. Wait for a case-specific checklist and agreed delivery instructions before sending anything.

Can documents be collected or delivered?

Ask about collection and return arrangements in your enquiry. Coverage, delivery method, charges and handling instructions must be agreed first.

Can I enquire from overseas?

Yes. State where you live and where your documents will be used so the appropriate process and any local attendance requirements can be reviewed.

Will I get application updates?

Agree how follow-ups and progress updates will be handled when the service is confirmed. Keep your enquiry reference when contacting DJP.
