Have another question? ↗
What does DJP stand for?

Document Journey Processing. We focus on visa expediting, Police Clearance Certificates (PCCs) and document assistance.

Can you guarantee my visa or completion date?

No. Decisions and processing times are controlled by the relevant authority. Available expediting options and realistic expectations must be confirmed for your individual case.

How much does the service cost?

Fees depend on the service and the assistance required. Submit an enquiry for a case-specific quote before committing to a service.

What should I send with my first enquiry?

Only your contact details, service, destination and deadline. Please do not include passport numbers, ID numbers, fingerprints or document scans in the enquiry form.

Can I enquire from outside South Africa?

Yes. Tell us where you are based and which country needs your documents so we can assess your request.
