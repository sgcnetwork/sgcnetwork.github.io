The learning garden

Learn Islam, one lesson at a time

Explore authentic Islamic knowledge with clear sources. These are introductory learning pathways; detailed lessons and verified scholarly contributions are being prepared.

Seerah · The life of the Prophet ﷺ

Begin with the early life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the first revelation, the Hijrah and his example of mercy. Read the Qur'an alongside reliable seerah works and teachers.

Sahabah · The companions

Study the courage, sacrifice and knowledge of Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, Ali and the many other companions, including the women among the Sahabah.

Muslim women in history

Discover Khadijah bint Khuwaylid, Aishah bint Abi Bakr and other influential Muslim women through carefully sourced biographies.

Hadith and everyday fiqh

Learn how hadith are authenticated, the importance of context, and why recognised schools of Islamic jurisprudence may differ. Seek qualified guidance for personal rulings.

Arabic beginnings

Learn the language of the Qur'an

Start with the letters and sounds, then progress to connected reading, common Qur'anic words and guided pronunciation.

ا ب ت

Lesson 1

Alif (ا), Bā' (ب) and Tā' (ت): recognise their shapes and learn their names.

َ ِ ُ

Lesson 2

Fathah, kasrah and dammah: discover how short vowel marks change pronunciation.

Audio pronunciation exercises and full structured courses are planned, not yet available.

Intentional scrolling

Guard My Gaze

A gentler approach to online life: favour beneficial reading, Qur'an, learning and thoughtful discussions over endless scrolling. BABM's community reporting and moderation remain important; no filter can guarantee every post is appropriate.

Try this: Set a reading intention before opening the feed, hide or report harmful posts, and take a break when scrolling stops being beneficial.

A reminder for your heart

My Heart Needs…

Choose a reflection to take back to the Qur'an.

Hope when I have made mistakes

“Do not despair of the mercy of Allah.” — Qur'an 39:53 (excerpt). Turn back to Allah with sincere tawbah and hope.

Comfort during hardship

“Indeed, with hardship comes ease.” — Qur'an 94:6. Reflect on Allah's promise while seeking the support you need.

Calm when my heart is unsettled

“Surely in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find comfort.” — Qur'an 13:28 (excerpt). Make time for dhikr and reflection.
