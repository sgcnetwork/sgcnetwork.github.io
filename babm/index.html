The learning garden

Learn Islam, one lesson at a time

Explore authentic Islamic knowledge with clear sources. These are introductory learning pathways; detailed lessons and verified scholarly contributions are being prepared.

Seerah · The life of the Prophet ﷺ

Begin with the early life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the first revelation, the Hijrah and his example of mercy. Read the Qur'an alongside reliable seerah works and teachers.

Sahabah · The companions

Study the courage, sacrifice and knowledge of Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, Ali and the many other companions, including the women among the Sahabah.

Muslim women in history

Discover Khadijah bint Khuwaylid, Aishah bint Abi Bakr and other influential Muslim women through carefully sourced biographies.

Hadith and everyday fiqh

Learn how hadith are authenticated, the importance of context, and why recognised schools of Islamic jurisprudence may differ. Seek qualified guidance for personal rulings.

BABM Arabic Academy · From the first letter

Learn the language of the Qur'an 🌷

Take your time, dear sister. Begin with the Arabic alphabet, practise the sounds and vowel marks, then build your confidence reading connected letters and familiar Qur'anic words.
