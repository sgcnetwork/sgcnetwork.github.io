03 / Human in control

Consultants approve. Automation assists.

Every case has a traceable decision, assigned owner and clear next action. AI never silently approves sensitive documentation.

01

Review queue

Consultant receives a structured pack with AI findings and source documents.

02

Verify evidence

Inspect originals, confirm flagged fields and apply destination rules.

03

Decide

Approve for next stage, request correction, reject or escalate.

04

Audit & notify

Record who decided, when, why and which client update was sent.

Approval safeguards

Role-based access · Two-person review for high-risk exceptions · Version history · Secure file access · POPIA-aware retention and consent · Human sign-off before submission.

Launch client references, PCC checklist, secure uploads, AI field extraction and consultant review.

PHASE 03

Measure and refine

PHASE 04

Expand carefully

Add visa country rules, translations, messaging, finance automation and management dashboards.
