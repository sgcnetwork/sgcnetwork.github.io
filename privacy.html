Scope and privacy contact

This notice covers personal information handled for the SGC Network website and services. For a privacy request, correction, deletion request or concern, contact netsgcwork@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”. Use only the information needed to describe your request; we may ask for proportionate verification before releasing account information.

Information we handle

  • Account information: username or name, email address, optional phone number and authentication records.
  • Orders and subscriptions: products, amounts, billing type, references, payment status and provider subscription identifiers. Card entry takes place with PayFast; SGC’s checkout integration does not collect your full card number or card security code.
  • Learning and community: access entitlements, progress, quiz or assessment responses, notes, submissions and posts you choose to provide.
  • Support and service work: enquiries, instructions, messages, business briefs and files you choose to supply.
  • Applications: contact details, application answers and CVs where you apply for an advertised role.
  • Technical information: information used to operate and protect the service, such as browser/network details, access logs, timestamps and error information.

Information needed to create an account, verify a purchase or supply a requested service is required for that purpose. Optional phone details or optional marketing information need not be supplied. Without necessary information, we may be unable to complete the relevant request.

Why information is used

We use relevant information to provide accounts and purchased access, process and reconcile payments, manage subscriptions, deliver learning and service work, respond to enquiries, handle applications, prevent misuse, resolve disputes and meet legal record-keeping obligations.

Processing relies on the grounds applicable to the purpose, including carrying out your agreement or requested steps, legal duties, legitimate operational interests where lawful, or consent where required. A Privacy Policy acknowledgement is not a blanket consent to unrelated uses.

Service providers and sharing

Relevant information is handled by providers needed to run the service: website hosting and delivery providers; Supabase for account, database and file services; PayFast for payments and recurring billing; and email-delivery or communication providers. If you contact us using Gmail, WhatsApp or another external channel, that channel also processes the communication under its own privacy terms.

We share information needed for the stated service, authorised professional assistance, a lawful request or the protection of legal rights. SGC does not sell your personal information. We do not publish your private account records merely because you join the community.

Information outside South Africa

Some hosting, database, authentication, email and communication providers operate outside South Africa, so relevant information may be stored or processed abroad. Our current Supabase project is hosted in the European Union. Cross-border processing must have an applicable lawful basis and appropriate protection; contact us for information about a transfer relevant to your request.

Cookies, local storage and optional tracking

The site uses browser storage and similar technologies for functional purposes such as keeping you signed in, retaining a cart or saving a demo selection. Hosting and external providers may also receive technical request information. Clearing browser storage can sign you out or remove a locally saved cart or preference.

Optional marketing or analytics must be explained and handled on an appropriate lawful basis, with a choice where required. An account or purchase is not an automatic opt-in to promotional messages. Where marketing is offered, you can refuse or withdraw it without losing an unrelated paid service.

Retention and security

Information is kept only for as long as needed for the stated purpose, the account or service relationship, applicable legal obligations or a legitimate dispute or security need. Different records can need different retention periods. A deletion request does not necessarily require deletion of transaction records that must lawfully be retained.

We use measures such as encrypted connections and access controls to protect information. No online service is risk-free. Tell us promptly about suspected account misuse; never send passwords or one-time codes to support. Where a security incident creates a legal notification duty, affected people and the regulator will be notified as required.

Your rights and complaints

Subject to the applicable law and verification, you may request access to information, correction or deletion, object to processing, or withdraw consent for processing that depends on consent. Explain your request at netsgcwork@gmail.com. We will explain any lawful reason why a request cannot be fulfilled in full.

You may raise a complaint with South Africa’s Information Regulator, including through POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za. You do not have to give up the right to approach the regulator in order to contact us first.

Children, other businesses and updates

If a child’s information is involved, the responsible adult must contact us before supplying it so the appropriate authority and safeguards can be established. Do not upload children’s or other people’s sensitive records into public community content.

Separately branded businesses or stores may be responsible for their own customer information and must supply their own applicable notices. Material changes to this notice will be published with an updated date and communicated where required.

Need help with an order or account? Email netsgcwork@gmail.com. Keep your order reference available, but never send a password, card PIN or one-time code.
