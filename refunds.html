Your rights come first

This policy applies to purchases from SGC Network. It does not replace rights under South African consumer law. A “digital product”, “joining fee” or “subscription” label does not by itself remove a refund or other remedy you are legally entitled to.

Stopping future subscription payments is different from requesting a refund of a payment already made. You can ask for both where appropriate.

How to request help or a refund

Email netsgcwork@gmail.com with the subject “Refund request” and include the account email, product or service, payment/order reference, payment date, the issue and the outcome you would like. A screenshot can help, but do not send card details, passwords or one-time codes. If you cannot access your email, contact WhatsApp support so we can arrange appropriate verification.

We will assess the purchase and explain the available remedy and any information still needed. Reporting an issue does not require you to make another payment.

Missing, incorrect or unusable delivery

If a payment is confirmed but an agreed file, access entitlement or service is missing, tell us so we can investigate and supply or correct it. If we cannot supply what was purchased, we will notify you and refund the undelivered item, unless you freely choose an agreed alternative. An alternative does not have to be accepted instead of a refund where you are entitled to one.

If a product is materially different from its description or has a defect, we will provide the remedy required by the circumstances and applicable law. We will not refuse a valid claim merely because a digital file was opened while you were checking it.

Cooling-off and statutory cancellation

Where section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act applies, a consumer can cancel without reason or penalty within seven days after receiving goods or concluding a service agreement. A qualifying refund is due within 30 days of cancellation. The Act contains exceptions, including certain services begun with the consumer’s consent during that period.

Applicability depends on the actual transaction; we do not treat every digital purchase as automatically excluded. Other consumer rights, including remedies for non-delivery or inadequate disclosures, remain available. Where supply is unavailable, we will notify you and refund as required, including the applicable statutory time limit.

Duplicates, incorrect amounts and unauthorised payments

Report a suspected duplicate charge, incorrect amount or unauthorised payment promptly. We will compare the provider reference and order record and correct a confirmed error. Contact your bank or payment provider promptly about suspected card misuse as well; this policy does not prevent you using legitimate provider dispute procedures.

A pending transaction is not always a settled charge. We will distinguish an authorisation, a completed payment and a refund before asking you to act.

Monthly access and change-of-mind requests

You can stop future automatic monthly charges using the Subscription & Cancellation Policy. Cancellation alone does not automatically refund a valid completed period; we assess a request under applicable law, the offer and what was supplied. This is not a blanket “no refunds” rule.

Where a change-of-mind request falls outside a legal cancellation right, tell us the circumstances. We will explain whether an additional refund or other resolution is available rather than assuming one is guaranteed.

Custom work and appointments

For custom websites, business setup, coaching and other agreed work, include the quote or booking reference. Any agreed cancellation terms must be supplied before purchase. We will consider the work actually authorised and performed and the costs lawfully recoverable; an unpublished or automatically forfeited fee will not override your statutory rights.

How an approved refund is paid

Refunds are normally sent through the original payment route where supported, with reasonable verification to protect against fraud. We will confirm the amount and processing status. Bank or provider posting times may affect when an initiated refund appears, but do not remove a statutory deadline.

Where a purchase is refunded, the access or licence for the refunded part may end as appropriate. We retain the transaction and refund record needed for accounting, legal obligations and dispute resolution rather than deleting the original purchase.

Need help with an order or account? Email netsgcwork@gmail.com. Keep your order reference available, but never send a password, card PIN or one-time code.
