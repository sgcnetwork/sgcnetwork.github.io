Three pieces, styled your way

Start with a silhouette you enjoy, add one considered accessory and let the details feel personal. The lookbook is inspiration; you can buy or style the pieces separately.

  • Choose a base piece you will wear often.
  • Add an accessory that works with your wardrobe.
  • Check each garment’s actual measurements.

A considered approach to sizing

Size labels vary between suppliers. Measure your bust, waist and hips and compare those measurements with the real garment chart before placing a live order.

  • Measure without pulling the tape tight.
  • Review fit notes and fabric composition.
  • Check the live store’s exchange and returns policy.

Building around everyday favourites

A small group of pieces can work across different plans when their colours and proportions feel right together. Start with what you already wear and add thoughtfully.

  • Consider your existing wardrobe colours.
  • Review fabric care before ordering.
  • Choose pieces for your actual everyday plans.
