A corner with a little character

A small group of considered objects can change the feeling of a room. Begin with the space you have, the light it receives and how you use it each day.

  • Measure surfaces and surrounding space.
  • Confirm product dimensions before ordering.
  • Layer objects without obstructing everyday use.

Choosing texture thoughtfully

Product photographs communicate a mood; actual material specifications tell you what you are buying. Review composition, finish and care information before a live purchase.

  • Look for verified materials and finishes.
  • Check cleaning and care instructions.
  • Compare colours in your actual room light.

Thinking about lighting

Lighting has both practical and decorative roles. Check a lamp’s electrical requirements, dimensions and supplier documentation alongside its appearance.

  • Review the actual electrical specifications.
  • Check plug, bulb and fitting requirements.
  • Confirm warranty and support terms with the supplier.
