Where can I track an order?

No real orders are placed in this showcase. Order tracking will be connected to the live business’s confirmed fulfilment process.

Where are delivery and returns policies?

The live business needs policies based on its actual supplier, courier and products before it accepts payment. This showcase does not present unverified delivery or refund promises as live terms.

Are favourites saved?

Yes. Favourites and your demonstration bag are saved in this browser where browser storage is available. They are not sent to a supplier or shared with other visitors.
