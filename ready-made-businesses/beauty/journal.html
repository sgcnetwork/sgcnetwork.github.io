A little ritual of your own

A beauty routine can be a moment to slow down. Before a live purchase, review the actual product’s ingredients, instructions and suitability rather than relying on the look of its packaging.

  • Read the verified ingredient and product information.
  • Follow the actual product’s usage guidance.
  • Choose products based on your own requirements.

What to look for on a product page

A useful beauty product page should include clear ingredients, size, directions and product-specific information. These details must come from a verified supplier.

  • Look for the exact product size.
  • Review ingredients and cautions.
  • Check storage and usage information.
