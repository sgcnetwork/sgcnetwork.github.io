A better technology shortlist

Start with the devices you already own and the task you want a product to support. A product name or photo does not establish compatibility.

  • Confirm connection standards and ports.
  • Check supported operating systems and devices.
  • Review the actual supplier’s model documentation.

Reading product specifications

Compare the same type of information across products. Look for verified specifications and separate those details from broad marketing descriptions.

  • Check battery and charging specifications.
  • Compare dimensions, weight and connection options.
  • Review warranty and after-sales support.

Choose around how you use it

Personal audio, shared audio and wearables serve different purposes. Begin with your use case, then compare the actual product specifications and support terms.

  • Describe the task you need to support.
  • Check the products that fit that task.
  • Confirm compatibility before ordering.
