Project Coordinator

Remote · South Africa · Commission only

Job description

Coordinate website and business automation projects, onboard clients, monitor deliverables, communicate with clients and identify business opportunities. Track timelines, communicate progress and support smooth project delivery from start to finish.

What you’ll do

  • Coordinate client projects and timelines.
  • Communicate with clients and the SGC Network team.
  • Track deliverables and follow up on project progress.
  • Support client onboarding and identify new opportunities.

Who we’re looking for

We're looking for someone organised, reliable, confident with clients and motivated to grow. Prior experience is advantageous, not essential.

Important remuneration disclosure: This opportunity is commission-based only at present, with no fixed monthly salary. Commission arrangements will be agreed in writing before work begins, subject to applicable employment law.
