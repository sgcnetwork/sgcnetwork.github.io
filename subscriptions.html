Subscriptions & Cancellation

How automatic monthly payments, access periods and cancellation work.

What is a subscription

A subscription is an offer explicitly marked for automatic recurring billing. PayFast handles the customer’s card authorisation. The initial amount and future recurring amount are shown before payment. A once-off joining fee or digital kit is not a recurring agreement unless a different billing arrangement is expressly agreed.

Monthly subscriptions run from period to period until cancelled; no fixed multi-month minimum is imposed by this policy. A separate custom-service agreement must disclose any different duration before you accept it.

Initial payment and renewals

After your first verified payment, the purchased access is activated. PayFast then processes the authorised monthly charges according to the agreement established at checkout. Check the PayFast subscription confirmation for the billing schedule. Every successfully verified renewal extends the applicable paid access.

Joining requirements, which features are included and any learning-release schedule must be stated in the offer. Subscription access is not a promise that all future material is already published.

Cancel future charges

  1. Sign in to your SGC account.
  2. Open Payments & subscriptions.
  3. Find the plan and choose Cancel subscription.
  4. Confirm the request and check for the cancellation result.

If the control fails or you cannot sign in, email netsgcwork@gmail.com with “Cancel subscription”, the account email and the plan name. We will verify the request and arrange cancellation. A sent message is not a provider confirmation; if a renewal is close, tell us and retain your request timestamp. An unsuccessful website control will not be used to deny an otherwise valid cancellation request.

What happens after cancellation

Once cancellation is confirmed with the provider, future recurring charges stop. Paid access normally continues until the end of the period already paid for. Cancelling does not delete your account or automatically refund an earlier payment. A payment already being processed may need separate reconciliation; tell us promptly if you are charged after an effective cancellation.

Deleting an app shortcut, signing out, abandoning an open checkout or stopping use of the website does not itself cancel an existing provider agreement. If you also want account deletion, make a separate privacy request.

Unsuccessful renewals and card changes

PayFast may retry unsuccessful payments and may suspend or lock an agreement under its process. A failed attempt does not create a new paid access period. When existing paid access expires, subscription features may be restricted until an appropriate successful payment or other agreed resolution.

Use the supported PayFast account or subscription-management route to update payment details, or contact us for guidance. Never send your card number, card security code, PIN or one-time code to SGC support. Do not start a second subscription just to correct an existing card without confirming that it will not duplicate your charges.

Price changes, pauses and different packages

A newly advertised price does not silently change the amount on your existing agreement. We will give any required notice and obtain the agreement required before changing an authorised recurring amount. If an existing plan needs to end, we will communicate the available options and respect the paid period and applicable rights.

Pausing, changing packages, reinstating an agreement or custom service arrangements need confirmation; do not assume they happen through an unrelated product purchase. Ready-made-business demo preferences do not establish a subscription.

Refunds and help

See the Refund Policy for missing access, duplicate payments, incorrect charges or statutory cancellation rights. For help with an agreement, email netsgcwork@gmail.com with its reference. Your rights under applicable consumer law take precedence over inconsistent wording in this policy.

Need help with an order or account? Email netsgcwork@gmail.com. Keep your order reference available, but never send a password, card PIN or one-time code.
