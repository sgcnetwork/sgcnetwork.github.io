2023–2028 Comprehensive Economic Development Strategy

From strategy to action

View the plan and public materials ↗

Prosperity for All is the region’s five-year economic development strategy. It guides investment and coordination across three priorities, with progress tracked through the CEDS annual report.

Economic opportunity

Expand access to opportunity

Connect workforce, education and business development priorities to regional action.

Infrastructure

Plan for regional growth

Coordinate investment in the infrastructure communities and employers rely on.

Resilience

Prepare for disruptions

Build the regional partnerships and planning capacity that support long-term recovery.
